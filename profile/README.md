## ◈ Who We Are

**Wendoro** is a technology company focused on building an integrated ecosystem of intelligent business management software, enterprise applications and digital infrastructure.

We believe business technology should be connected, adaptable and accessible.

Our vision is to simplify complex business operations through modular software, intelligent automation and thoughtfully engineered digital experiences.

## ✧ Our Technology Philosophy

We build with a focus on:

- **Modularity** — Independent components that work together.
- **Scalability** — Architecture designed to grow with businesses.
- **Interoperability** — APIs and integrations across platforms.
- **Security** — Responsible engineering and data protection.
- **Intelligence** — Practical AI and automation.
- **Extensibility** — Flexible systems that developers can build upon.

## ⌘ Engineering & Open Source

We believe in building a strong technology ecosystem through engineering collaboration.

Our development approach combines:

- Proprietary enterprise products.
- Selected open-source projects and utilities.
- Developer tools and SDKs.
- Integration frameworks.
- Community-driven contributions.

Each project follows its own licensing and contribution policy. Please check the individual repository for its license and development guidelines.

## ◎ Our Mission

To empower businesses with connected, intelligent and adaptable technology.

We want organizations to spend less time managing disconnected systems and more time building, innovating and growing.
