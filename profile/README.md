# Wendoro

### One Platform. Every Business.

Wendoro is a technology company building an integrated ecosystem of business management software and intelligent digital solutions.

We aim to simplify how businesses operate, collaborate, manage resources and grow through connected, scalable and extensible technology.

### Our Ecosystem

- **ERP** — Enterprise resource planning
- **CRM** — Customer relationship management
- **HR & Workforce** — Human resources and workforce management
- **Finance** — Financial and accounting management
- **Production** — Manufacturing and operational management
- **AI & Automation** — Intelligent workflows and business automation
- **Developer Platform** — APIs, integrations and extensibility

### Our Engineering Principles

- Modular and scalable architecture
- API-first development
- Security and reliability
- Open standards and interoperability
- Responsible AI integration
- Extensible business applications

### Our Mission

To make powerful business technology accessible, connected and adaptable for businesses of every size.
